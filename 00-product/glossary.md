# Glossary

**Project:** Banking Legal Platform

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official terminology used throughout the Banking Legal Platform.

Every architecture document, ADR, API specification, database schema and source code should follow the definitions in this glossary.

If a term exists in this document, its meaning should not be redefined elsewhere.

---

# A

## Answer

The final response generated by the AI system.

An Answer must always be generated from retrieved evidence and should never rely solely on the language model's internal knowledge.

---

## Audit Log

A chronological record of important system events.

Examples include:

- User login
- Document upload
- OCR execution
- Parser execution
- Search request
- AI response generation

---

# C

## Chunk

A retrieval unit generated from one or more Knowledge Tree nodes.

Chunks exist only to improve retrieval performance.

Chunks are derived data and are NOT the source of truth.

Multiple chunking strategies may exist for the same document.

---

## Citation

Human-readable information that identifies the original location of retrieved evidence.

Examples:

- Circular 12/2024
- Article 15
- Clause 2
- Page 17

Citation exists for transparency and verification.

---

## Collection

A logical group of documents inside a Vault.

Collections are used for organization only.

---

# D

## Department Vault

A shared storage space accessible by members of the same department.

Documents stored in a Department Vault inherit the department's access permissions.

---

## Document

A legal or internal banking document managed by the platform.

Supported examples include:

- Laws
- Decrees
- Circulars
- Decisions
- Internal regulations
- Internal policies

A Document is represented by one or more Document Versions.

---

## Document Version

A specific revision of a Document.

Different versions may have different:

- Effective dates
- Content
- Relationships
- Legal status

Each version maintains its own Knowledge Tree.

---

# E

## Evidence

The document fragments actually used by the AI system to generate an Answer.

Evidence is produced by the retrieval pipeline.

Evidence should always be traceable back to the original document.

Evidence and Citation are different concepts.

---

# K

## Knowledge Tree

The structured representation of a Document Version.

The Knowledge Tree preserves the logical hierarchy of the original document.

Examples of node types include:

- Chapter
- Section
- Article
- Clause
- Point
- Appendix

The Knowledge Tree is the source of truth for the platform.

Chunks, embeddings and citations are all derived from the Knowledge Tree.

---

## Knowledge Tree Node

A single element inside the Knowledge Tree.

Every node contains:

- Original text
- Position within the document
- Parent node
- Child nodes
- Metadata

Nodes are immutable after creation.

---

# L

## Logical Document

The conceptual identity of a legal document regardless of its versions.

Example:

Circular 12/2024

may contain multiple revisions over time while remaining the same Logical Document.

---

# M

## Metadata

Structured information describing a document.

Typical metadata includes:

- Title
- Document type
- Issuing authority
- Issue date
- Effective date
- Status
- Security level
- Applicable subjects
- Exceptions

Metadata is extracted during ingestion and may be refined later.

---

# O

## Original Document

The uploaded PDF or DOCX file.

The Original Document is immutable.

It is the legal source from which all derived data is generated.

No processing step may modify its content.

---

# P

## Parser

A component responsible for converting an Original Document into a Knowledge Tree.

The parser preserves document structure without altering document meaning.

---

## Personal Vault

Private storage owned by an individual user.

Only the owner (and authorized administrators) may access its contents.

---

# R

## Retrieval

The process of locating relevant Evidence from indexed documents.

Retrieval may combine:

- Keyword search
- Semantic search
- Metadata filtering

---

# V

## Vault

A secure logical storage space for documents.

The system currently supports:

- Personal Vault
- Department Vault

Vaults define ownership and access boundaries.

---

# Version Timeline

The chronological history of all versions belonging to the same Logical Document.

The timeline allows users to inspect historical revisions and identify the currently effective version.

---

# Design Rules

The following rules apply across the entire platform.

1. The Original Document is immutable.

2. The Knowledge Tree is the source of truth.

3. Chunks are derived from the Knowledge Tree.

4. Evidence is used by the AI.

5. Citation is presented to the user.

6. Every Answer should be traceable back to the Original Document.

7. When evidence is insufficient, the system should abstain rather than hallucinate.
