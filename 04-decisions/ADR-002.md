# ADR-002

## Title

Evidence-First Generation

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Date

2026-08-06

---

# Context

Large Language Models are capable of generating fluent and convincing responses.

However, legal information systems require answers that are:

- verifiable
- traceable
- reproducible
- grounded in official documents

Allowing the language model to answer from its internal knowledge introduces unacceptable risks:

- hallucination
- outdated regulations
- missing citations
- inconsistent answers

The platform therefore requires a generation strategy that prioritizes evidence over model knowledge.

---

# Decision

Generation shall always be evidence-driven.

The Generation module consumes only the Retrieval Contract.

Generation shall never retrieve documents directly.

Generation shall never access vector databases.

Generation shall never read original documents.

Every factual statement presented to the user shall originate from retrieved evidence.

The role of the language model is to explain, organize and present evidence rather than create new knowledge.

---

# Consequences

## Positive

Answers remain traceable.

Citations remain consistent.

Different language models produce comparable results.

Prompt engineering becomes simpler.

Generation becomes replaceable.

Legal audit becomes possible.

---

## Negative

Answer quality depends heavily on Retrieval quality.

Poor retrieval cannot be compensated for by the language model.

Generation may refuse to answer when evidence is insufficient.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Alternative A

LLM answers directly from its pretrained knowledge.

Rejected.

Reason:

Knowledge may be outdated or unverifiable.

---

## Alternative B

LLM retrieves documents autonomously using tools.

Rejected.

Reason:

Retrieval logic becomes embedded inside prompts.

System behaviour becomes difficult to reproduce and benchmark.

---

## Alternative C

Generation consumes Retrieval Contract only.

Accepted.

Reason:

Retrieval and Generation remain independently testable.

Every answer remains explainable.

---

# Architectural Impact

Generation depends only on:

- Retrieval Contract

Generation never depends on:

- PDF files
- OCR output
- parser output
- vector database
- search engine

Retrieval is solely responsible for evidence selection.

Generation is solely responsible for presentation.

---

# Invariants

Every factual statement originates from retrieved evidence.

Generation never invents citations.

Generation never accesses original documents.

Generation never performs retrieval.

Retrieval never generates answers.

Evidence remains immutable throughout generation.

---

# Future Considerations

Future language models may improve explanation quality.

They may improve formatting.

They may improve summarization.

They shall not change the principle that evidence precedes generation.

---

# Rationale

Separating retrieval from generation creates clear module boundaries.

The Retrieval module determines *what is relevant*.

The Generation module determines *how to communicate it*.

This separation improves explainability, testing, reproducibility and long-term maintainability.

The language model acts as an interpreter of evidence rather than an authority on legal knowledge.
